Most traditional answering services—live operators on a call center floor—are built for doctors' offices and law firms. They take a name, number, and brief note. A plumber calls back later. That model loses emergency jobs because the customer has already called three other plumbers by then.

The modern answer is a [24/7 AI answering service that dispatches immediately](/blog/ai-answering-service). That is job capture. Everything else is just voicemail.

## What Should a Plumbing Answering Service Do on an Emergency Call?

The right answering service does five things in under five minutes:

1. **Answer instantly** — no queue, no hold music. A panicked homeowner doesn't wait. If you don't pick up in 3 rings, 85% will hang up and call the next plumber.

2. **Triage the emergency** — ask the right questions to understand urgency. Is it a burst pipe (dispatch immediately) or a slow leak (can it wait 24 hours)? Is there active water damage? What's the property address?

3. **Confirm customer authorization** — get permission to send a technician, confirm the address, get a callback number.

4. **Route the dispatch** — send a push notification to your on-call plumber with all the details. No SMS delays. No manual callback list. Instant.

5. **Confirm arrival time** — tell the customer the tech's name and exact arrival window before the call ends. The customer knows help is coming. They stop calling other plumbers.

Traditional answering services skip steps 4 and 5 entirely. They take a message and promise to call you back with a message. The customer is long gone by then.
